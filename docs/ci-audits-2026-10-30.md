# Continuous compatibility audits / 持续兼容性检查

## Changes / 更新

Ubuntu R-release now installs CI-only haven, units and bit64 dependencies. After
the normal package check it compiles a separate installed copy into a temporary
library, then runs docs/system-audit.R and docs/import-types-audit.R in separate
Rscript processes. Missing dependencies and nonzero child exits fail the job;
the step has a 15-minute timeout. The other four matrix jobs are unchanged.

Ubuntu R-release 已自动运行扩大一致性及导入类型检查，使用独立安装的包，
不是 pkgload 开发命名空间。缺失依赖或子检查失败会使 CI 失败，不会静默跳过。
这些依赖仅用于 CI，DESCRIPTION、解析代码、六个接口和性能开销没有变化。

Three bilingual documentation discrepancies were corrected: matrices/arrays
are rejected, accounting intervals share suffixes bidirectionally, and the
`3万-abc` example reports its specific right-endpoint failure. These correct
descriptions of existing behavior, not three new production parsing defects.
